## Full notice

**Who we are.** Optomate LTD, company number [COMPANY_NUMBER], registered office [REGISTERED_OFFICE]. We decide how
your data is used (we are the "controller"). For any data request, email **admin@optomate.co.uk**.
Data protection officer: *none appointed (TO CONFIRM)*.

**Why we have your details.** We study how HVAC firms in Dallas–Fort Worth handle calls, and we may offer a
call-answering service to a small number of them by email. To decide whom to contact, we may make one short call and
send one message through your website's contact form, at most once a month each. Both say who we are and ask one
question: whether you take emergency calls after 6pm. We note whether the call was answered and how quickly the form
got a reply.

**When you get this notice.** When we first speak to you on the phone or message you through your website form, and
in our first email, which we send within 1 month of collecting your data. If a call is not answered, we leave no
message.

**Our legal basis.** Legitimate interests: Optomate LTD's interest in finding businesses that miss customer calls and
offering them a call-answering service by business-to-business email. We contact only LLC and Inc businesses, never
sole traders.

**What we hold about your business.**
- business name, TDLR licence number, licence expiry date and county;
- the business phone number, contact-form page and email address (named or general) published on your own website,
  each with the page it came from, and your website address;
- the name and role of a named owner or manager (owner, co-owner, founder, president, CEO or general manager),
  only as shown on your own website, with the page it came from;
- facts about your business taken from your own website or the TDLR register to personalise our first email, each
  with its source page;
- map-listing IDs (a Google place ID and IDs from open map datasets);
- the result of our call (answered, voicemail, no answer, or line not working), its date and time, and whether you
  asked us not to call again;
- when our form message was sent and when it got its first reply;
- a simple score (0 to 3) built from the call and form results. It only decides whether we email you. It has no legal
  or similarly significant effect on you.

The licence register also lists the licence holder's name. We do not use it, and we are removing it from our records.

**What we never collect or use.** Email addresses that are guessed, built from a pattern or bought; personal mobile
numbers; review text; anything collected automatically from social media; and call recordings. We do not record calls.

**Where it comes from.** Public business registers (TDLR), the business's own website, and open map datasets. All are
publicly available. We can give the exact names on request.

**Who receives it.**
- **SuperSend** sends our emails (United States).
- **Google** receives business-name searches through the Places API.
- **Stripe UK** processes payments, only if you buy a pilot.

Only Ahmed, at Optomate, sees the lists and makes the calls. The data is stored on Ahmed's Mac. Our code is kept on
GitHub, but your data is not.

**Transfers outside the UK.** SuperSend is in the United States. That transfer is protected by *[UK Data Bridge /
IDTA / UK Addendum: TO CONFIRM from SuperSend's DPA]*.

**How long we keep it.** We keep your data for 6 months after our last contact with you, then delete it. If you ask
us not to contact you, we keep only the minimum needed to recognise your business, permanently, and only so that we
never contact you again.

**Your rights.** You can:
- **object** at any time. We then stop all contact, with no questions asked;
- **see** the data we hold (access);
- **have it deleted** (erasure);
- have it corrected, or limit how we use it.

Email admin@optomate.co.uk, or tell us on a call.

**Complaints.** You can complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office: https://ico.org.uk.
